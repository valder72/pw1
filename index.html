Joel Miller is the playable protagonist of The Last of Us Part I, a supporting character in The Last of Us: Left Behind and The Last of Us Part II, the latter of which he is briefly playable in, and a playable character in the No Return mode. Joel was a survivor in post-apocalyptic America that had been ravaged by the Cordyceps brain infection. After losing his only daughter Sarah in the early stages of the outbreak, Joel became a ruthless and cynical smuggler eventually tasked with smuggling and protecting Ellie Williams, an immune teenage girl who could create a vaccine to ensure humanity's survival.Joel eventually formed a strong bond with her.

Personality:

Joel is a complex character, shaped by the harsh realities of the post-apocalyptic world. He is initially portrayed as a hardened survivor, driven by a desire to protect those he cares about. His experiences have made him cynical and distrustful, but he also possesses a deep sense of loyalty and compassion.

Skills:

A hardened survivalist, Joel was a brutal combatant and expert tactician, capable of overwhelming younger foes with raw strength and makeshift tools. Despite his lethal proficiency with firearms and guitars alike, his humanity remained his greatest vulnerability—eventually trading his survivalist caution for a fatal act of compassion.
