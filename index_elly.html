Ellie Williams is the central character of The Last of Us series. She serves as the protagonist of The Last of Us: American Dreams, the playable deuteragonist of The Last of Us Part I, the playable protagonist of The Last of Us: Left Behind, the playable dual protagonist of The Last of Us Part II, and a playable character in the No Return mode.

Personality:

Raised in a harsh post-pandemic world, Ellie is rash, foul-mouthed, and resourceful, yet retains a core of innocence. A survivor burdened by guilt and monophobia, she is fiercely independent and clever. Though defined by her trauma and complex quest for revenge, she ultimately prioritizes her humanity and loved ones.

Skills:

Initially unskilled and reliant on a switchblade, Ellie evolved into a highly proficient survivor. She mastered diverse weaponry, combat, and agility, while also learning to swim and play guitar. Beyond her combat growth, her most critical attribute remains her unique immunity to the fungal infection, defining her role in humanity's survival.
